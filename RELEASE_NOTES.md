## ST1M PORT4L 1.2 — competition hub, pro profiles, stretched res, new look

### New
- **Pro profiles** — 15 keyboard-and-mouse setups (movement specialists included) with photos. Click a player and choose exactly what to add to your draft: resolution & FOV, graphics, Reflex & V-Sync, movement keys, launch options. Sources and update dates shown on every card; anything the app can't write exactly is listed as "not applied" instead of guessed.
- **Stretched resolution, no black bars** — pick a 4:3 / 16:10 profile and launch Apex from the app. It switches your display for the game session and restores it automatically when Apex closes. Includes a **live preview** on a real gameplay clip (native vs stretched, with or without black bars) and adds `+mat_letterbox_aspect_min 1.0` for you.
- **My Apex profile & Top Predator** — rank, RP, ladder position, lifetime stats, an RP-over-time chart and a "RP left to Predator" gauge (uses your own free [Apex Legends Status API](https://apexlegendsapi.com/) key, stored encrypted on your PC). Add friends or rivals to a local RP ladder.
- **New look** — one glass panel, a plain-text menu, big condensed typography, smooth page transitions, a launch splash, and an animated Outlands-style landscape background (a video background option is also available).
- **Your own background video** (1.2.2) — System → Appearance → Choose video. The file stays on your PC (copied to the app's data folder), it is never uploaded or bundled.
- **English by default** — switch to French in System → Appearance.

### Fixed
- Key names written to `settings.cfg` now match what the game uses (`LSHIFT`, `LCTRL`, `MWHEELUP`…) and custom binds are flagged like the game does — this fixes wheel-based bind packs (tap strafe, scroll jump…).
- Previews now use **real in-game captures only** (the generated placeholder scene is gone).

### Notes
- Windows only. Unsigned installer: SmartScreen may ask for confirmation.
- Not affiliated with EA / Respawn. Pro settings are transcribed from public ProSettings.net pages and change often.
